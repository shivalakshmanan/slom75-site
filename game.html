Game

Lights On

Twelve towers have lost their power. Turn the wiring until every room connects back to the mains. There’s no clock and no way to lose.

Tower 1 of 12

mains

How to play
  • Tap or click a tile to turn it a quarter-turn clockwise. Shift-click or right-click turns it the other way.
  • Power starts at the mains, the ringed tile on the ground floor. A room lights up once its wire connects back to it.
  • Light every room to finish the tower. Each tower is a little bigger than the last.
  • On a keyboard, move with the arrow keys and press Enter or Space to turn a tile.
  • Stuck? “Show a hint” turns one tile the right way and pins it there.
